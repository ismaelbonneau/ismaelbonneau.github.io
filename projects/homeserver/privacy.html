Privacy Policy

homeserver · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how homeserver (the "app"), published by Ismaël Bonneau, handles the data of users who connect it to their Google account.

1. Data the app accesses

Homeserver Security Alerts is a private application used to send automated notifications from a personal home server. It does not read, search, or modify emails in the user's mailbox.

OAuth scopes requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.

2. How the data is used

The application uses OAuth 2.0 authentication to send emails explicitly requested by the server's notification scripts. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the server and are not shared with third parties.

3. Data sharing

The application does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

The app does not store the content of the emails it sends beyond what is strictly necessary to send them. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us at ismael.homeserver@gmail.com.

5. Security

The app only requests permission to send emails on your behalf. It cannot read, search, modify or delete any message in your mailbox, nor access your contacts or any other Google data.

Communication between your browser, the app and Google's servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your Google account is granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow: the app never sees or stores your Google password. OAuth tokens are kept only for as long as needed to send the emails you request, are protected against unauthorized access, and are deleted when you revoke access. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Compliance with Google's policy

homeserver's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Contact

For any question or request (access, deletion): ismael.homeserver@gmail.com.
